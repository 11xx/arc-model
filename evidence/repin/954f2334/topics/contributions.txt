Contribution roles and canonical Git trailers

CONTRIBUTION TRAILERS
    arc roles <change> --planned-by [<value>] --orchestrated-by [<value>]
                       Assert roles on an open change. Both flags repeat;
                       at least one assertion or withdrawal is required.
                       --withdraw <role>=<value> withdraws an exact assertion.
                       Values use the convention's agent or person form.
                       `Planned-by` and `Orchestrated-by` credit work done for
                       at least one other participant; a single-participant
                       commit carries only `Implemented-by`.
                       A flag with no value stands for the invoking
                       identity: a declared Name <email> actor's own form,
                       else harness:model from the resolved harness and
                       model, under a declared actor or one derived from the
                       harness session. A role assertion is no approval, so
                       a derived actor may make one. An identity assumed
                       from git config, or an unresolved harness or model,
                       refuses: omit the role or pass a real coordinate.
                       A value, typed or resolved, that carries a
                       placeholder or a session id refuses; withdrawal takes
                       any recorded value.
                       Assertions are explicit; event ancestry grants none.
                       begin's two role flags and brief --planned-by take the
                       same forms. Put the change first when a role flag has
                       no value, or attach an explicit coordinate with
                       --planned-by=<value> or --orchestrated-by=<value>.
                       A role value resolving to a repository change refuses.
                       begin records the assertions directly
                       after opening, brief when writing a contract.
    arc trailers <change> [--json]
                       Print Planned-by and Orchestrated-by assertions,
                       Implemented-by from recorded patchset contributors
                       (or the snapshot subject when none are listed), and
                       Reviewed-by from qualifying pre-integration Approved
                       verdicts covering the current or shipped patchset, in
                       convention order. Duplicate lines print once.
                       The subject is --on-behalf-of when set, else the actor.
                       A subject that is a fresh harness:model[#effort] or
                       Name <email> supplies itself. Retained replay or
                       bound-run author coordinates take precedence over
                       session names. The recorder's harness and model apply
                       only to the recorder's own work, on a snapshot that declared its attribution: a declared
                       actor, --solo, --contributors or --on-behalf-of. A
                       recorder inferred from its harness session or Git
                       configuration is not credited, since who ran snapshot
                       is not proof of who wrote the diff.
                       Pre-integration qualifying Approved verdicts whose
                       patchset `approved_head` matches the resolved current
                       (for an open change) or shipped head project
                       `Reviewed-by: <coordinate>` from `on_behalf_of`, a
                       declared person, or `harness:model`; an open change
                       whose branch head is unreadable omits `Reviewed-by`.
                       `comment-only`, `changes-requested`, findings-only
                       non-approvals, rejected self-approvals, debt waivers,
                       and post-integration `arc audit` discharges never
                       project `Reviewed-by`; audits remain ledger-only and
                       are never backfilled into already-integrated history.
                       No line carries a placeholder or a session id. What
                       cannot be attributed is omitted and named in a note
                       with the value, why (placeholder, session id,
                       unresolved, undeclared) and the remedy; no coordinate
                       is guessed. A note on a session-form contributor may
                       name the one model this change's own records by that
                       actor carry, as a suggestion that is never projected.
                       An asserted role that is no fresh coordinate becomes
                       a note naming arc roles --withdraw.
                       Review snapshots and squash use the same rule;
                       rewrites retain patchset attribution.
                       --json emits arc-trailers/1 with trailers and notes.
    check and integrate advise when a commit in target..head lacks any
    projected contribution trailer (`Planned-by`, `Implemented-by`,
    `Reviewed-by`, `Orchestrated-by`) applicable to that commit's resulting
    block, under missing-contribution-trailers. By default (`[trailers] auto =
    true`), `arc integrate` and `arc land` auto-stamp missing applicable
    trailers at integration time (on `target..head` via `role_additions` for
    fast-forward / `arc land`, or onto the merge commit message for `--shape
    no-ff`; `--no-trailers` or `[trailers] auto = false` opts out). For PRs,
    contribution repos, or manual pre-integration stamping, the advisory names
    `arc rewrite trailers --sync` (and `--from <rev> --append <line>` only
    when every advised line applies to every commit that bulk rewrite would
    touch); only fresh coordinates are advised, and both auto-stamping and
    `--sync` omit lines that would introduce a single-participant violation on
    that commit. They advise when a commit's role line
    carries a placeholder or a session id, or a person without an email, under
    placeholder-contribution-trailers, naming each commit and line and arc
    rewrite trailers --from <rev> --edits <file>, whose `arc-trailer-edits/1`
    lines drop or replace exactly those lines and leave the valid lines under
    the same key. Neither advice blocks
    integration or changes ready or the exit code. Appending complete role
    entries preserves approvals, debt waivers, commit-bound gates, and
    matching budget raises, and the appended text is a declaration, not a
    verdict; replacing or dropping trailers with `--edits` or any other
    message rewrite requires fresh snapshot and approval or declared debt
    (`arc guide rewrites-evidence`).
    Post-integration audits stay in the ledger.
    arc instructions git [--check <file>]
                       Print the portable contribution-trailer convention
                       `arc-contribution-trailers/2`: `Planned-by`,
                       `Implemented-by`, `Reviewed-by`, and `Orchestrated-by`
                       name material contributions in agent
                       `harness:model[#effort]` or human `Name <email>` form.
                       `Planned-by` and `Orchestrated-by` credit work done for
                       at least one other participant; a single-participant
                       commit carries only `Implemented-by`.
                       A placeholder or a session id is not a coordinate;
                       its line is omitted.
                       Agent harness, model and supplied effort segments
                       must be nonempty.
                       `--check <file>` reports malformed role values,
                       placeholder role values (a placeholder or session-id
                       segment, or a person without an email), advisory
                       single-participant misuse (`Planned-by` whose
                       participant is the sole `Implemented-by`, or
                       `Orchestrated-by` with no `Implemented-by` from a
                       different participant), and keys outside the
                       convention. It never rewrites a message, and exits 1
                       when a role key carries a malformed or placeholder
                       value.

    Gate trailers (`arc-gate-trailers/1`):
    Commits in a change's `merge-base..head` range (against its target branch,
    never a stale recorded base) may declare gate inputs in their final trailer
    block:
      Witness: <name>             one to three distinct single-token witness
                                  names, deduplicated in first-seen order
      Witness: none (<REASON>)    explicit exemption with a non-empty
                                  parenthesised reason; cannot mix with named
                                  witnesses
      Change-size: interface      selects a gate's declared `interface_budget`;
                                  any other value is refused
    Invalid, empty, multiline, mixed, or excessive (> 3 distinct) declarations
    refuse `arc verify`, `arc check`, `arc integrate`, and `arc land`, and
    report as `gate-trailers-invalid` in `arc doctor`.
    Valid declarations bind into verification-reuse identity, so changing
    `Witness:` or `Change-size:` trailers invalidates prior passes even at an
    unchanged tree. Executed gates receive `ARC_WITNESSES` (newline-separated
    witness names or `none (<REASON>)`) and `ARC_CHANGE_SIZE` when declared;
    both are unset when absent. Target-branch invariant list entries from a
    gate's `invariants` file are passed in `ARC_INVARIANTS` (unset when absent).
